Delete your data

AquaMind (Android app com.mikpo.aquamind) by Mikpo Studio

AquaMind has no accounts and no servers. Everything you log is stored only on your phone, so you can delete it yourself at any time, without contacting us.

Delete everything in the app

  1. Open AquaMind and go to the Settings tab.
  2. Tap Privacy & ads.
  3. Tap Delete all data and confirm.

This immediately and permanently deletes:

  • all logged drinks (amounts, times and vessel types),
  • your settings (daily goal, units, wake and bed time, reminder preferences and your optional first name),
  • technical app state, such as streaks and ad frequency counters,
  • all scheduled reminders.

Nothing is kept. Uninstalling AquaMind also deletes all of this data from your phone.

Advertising data

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Mikpo Studio does not receive or store advertising data. To limit or stop it:

  • In AquaMind: Settings → Privacy & ads → Privacy choices (where available) to change or withdraw your consent.
  • On your phone: Settings → Google → Ads to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Google's own retention and deletion practices are described at policies.google.com/privacy.

Questions

If you can't open the app or have any question about your data, email mikpostudio@outlook.com. Because we don't hold your hydration data, we can't delete it for you, but we're happy to help.
