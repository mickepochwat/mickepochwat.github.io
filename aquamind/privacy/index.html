Privacy Policy

AquaMind (Android app com.mikpo.aquamind) · Effective date: 7 October 2026

Developer: Mikpo Studio · Contact: mikpostudio@outlook.com

1. Summary

AquaMind is a water reminder and hydration tracker. It works without an account. Your hydration entries and settings are stored only on your device. The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which processes some device data as described in section 4.

2. Data stored on your device

  • The amounts, times and vessel types of drinks you log.
  • Your settings: daily goal, units, wake and bed time, reminder preferences and, optionally, your first name for the greeting.
  • Technical app state, such as how often ads may be shown.

This data stays in the app's private storage on your device. We never receive it. It is removed when you use Settings → Privacy & ads → Delete all data or uninstall the app. You can export your history as a CSV file; where that file goes is up to you and the app you share it with.

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, AquaMind schedules reminders locally on your device, within the active hours you choose. No push server is involved and no notification data is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in the app or in your device settings.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

AquaMind uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show ads, and Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland). Depending on your choices and device settings, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the advertising ID,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about your interactions with ads, and diagnostic data,

for serving and measuring ads, analytics and fraud prevention. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. More about how Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices: Settings → Privacy & ads → Privacy choices (shown where applicable) lets you review or change your consent. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in the device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads; wording varies by device). Declining consent does not limit any AquaMind feature.

5. Analytics

AquaMind does not send analytics to us or to any analytics provider.

6. Children

AquaMind is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

7. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Storing your own data locally is necessary to provide the app you asked for. Advertising based on personal data relies on your consent where required, collected through Google's consent message.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of personal data. Because we do not receive your hydration data, you control it directly in the app. For advertising data, use the choices in section 4 or contact Google. For any question, email mikpostudio@outlook.com.

9. Security

Data is kept in the app's sandboxed storage on your device, protected by your device's own security (screen lock, encryption).

10. Changes

We will update this policy if the app's data practices change, and revise the effective date above.
